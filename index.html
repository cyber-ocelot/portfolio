About

Curiosity as
a way of life.

I'm a rising sophomore at Neuqua Valley High School with my sights set on aerospace engineering. Mysteries surround us; from the familiar revving of engines in a garage to the star that rises and sets in our sky everyday. I have my mind set on traversing this jagged terrain.

My life revolves around science and the way the world works - everything has a reason.

The other half of my life has been committed to violin since 2024. When words leave off, music begins.

Outside of music and STEM, I write, read, and make art - hobbies I make time for in my busy schedule. I've found myself volunteering increasingly, helping a range of individuals - from my own peers to children abroad.

Argonne National Laboratory

Science,
up close.

Summer 2024
CodeGirls@Argonne
A week-long summer camp at Argonne National Laboratory where each day brought a different corner of computer science to life — from Python fundamentals to the principles of quantum mechanics. The week culminated in a paired project showcase where participants applied what they'd learned to build something of their own.
Fall 2024
Introduce a Girl to Engineering Day
Selected as one of 100 students for a full day immersed in Argonne's scientific community. Paired with a nuclear engineer as my mentor, I toured one of three Argonne research facilities, attended a showcase where scientists explained their active projects, and competed in a car-building challenge.

Given a limited set of materials, my partner and I methodically tested several designs — weighing tradeoffs between speed and stability — before settling on the one we were most confident in.
2nd place in the racing competition
Summer 2025
Science Discovery Camp
A competitive, week-long program for science-enthusiastic teens. Selected through a rigorous application process, I explored the breadth of research happening at Argonne through expert-led tours and hands-on activities.
Monday · Energy
Built battery-powered cars using homemade zinc-copper electrodes. Toured the Transportation Energy Lab.
Tuesday · AI & Cosmology
Explored ImageNet and AI vision. Built a cardboard spectroscope. Toured cosmology labs — including equipment from the South Pole Telescope.
Wednesday · Quantum
Learned about superposition and quantum networks. Toured the Center for Nanoscale Materials — clean rooms, silicon wafers, and real quantum computers.
Friday · Showcase
Built a physical model of Schrödinger's Cat in a Box to demonstrate quantum superposition — and designed a poster explaining the thought experiment.

Music

The other
language.

"Violin is the one part of my life that has nothing to prove."
It doesn't need to connect to aerospace or physics — though if you think about it long enough, the acoustics are fascinating. Practicing is where I go when I need to stop analyzing and just listen. There's something about the discipline of it — the slow accumulation of something difficult becoming easy — that quietly makes me better at everything else.
